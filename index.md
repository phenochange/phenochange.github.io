<section class="intro-section section">
  <div class="shell two-column">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">What is PhenoChange?</p>
      <h2>Watching seasonal change, one tree (or grass) at a time.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="prose-large">
      <p>The ecosystem function of tropical forests and savannas depends critically on vegetative phenology: the timing of leaf production and loss.</p>
      <p>PhenoChange uses ground-based camera systems to monitor how these seasonal rhythms are shifting under global change.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
