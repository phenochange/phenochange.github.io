# Protocols

PhenoChange develops standardized protocols for the installation, operation and analysis of ground-based camera systems used to monitor the phenology of tropical forests and savannas. These protocols are intended to ensure that data collected across the network are directly comparable and reproducible.

---

## Protocols for downstream image processing and data analysis

**Analysis pipelines are currently in development.**

Future releases will include standardized workflows for:

- image quality control
- region of interest (ROI) delineation
- extraction of vegetation indices
- calculation of phenological metrics
- integration with Earth Observation products
